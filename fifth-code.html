Chicken Biryani Recipe

This delicious chicken biryani is ready in just 45 minuets. This chicken biryani is perfect for 4 peoples.

Ingredients

  • Rise - 1/2 kg
  • Chicken - 1 kg
  • Biryani Masala - 1 packet
  • Oil - 2 Cups

Instructions

  1. First, soak the rise for 20 minuets.
  2. Heat oil in a pot and add chicken and cook for 15 minuets.
  3. Add biryani masala and soaked rise and water.
  4. Leave it for 20 minuets and your chicken biryani is ready to serve.
